# 📖 User Guide: Strategies & Interpretation

This application offers different "Strategies" (Metrics) to visualize your data. Here is how to interpret each one.

## ⚡ Key Feature: Auto-Skip (Fast Forward)
If you took a break from Dota 2 (e.g., no games for 6 months), the video will automatically **skip** those months. You will see the date counter jump (e.g., from "June 2018" to "January 2019"). This keeps the video pacing fast and engaging.

---

## 📊 Core Strategies

### 1. Matches Played
* **What it shows:** A simple count of how many games you've played on each hero.
* **Interpretation:** The "Main" race. Shows your favorite heroes over time.

### 2. Total Wins
* **What it shows:** Total number of victories per hero.
* **Interpretation:** Compares clearly with "Matches Played." If a hero is high on Matches but low on Wins, that's your "Guilty Pleasure" hero.

### 3. Win Rate % (Top 20 Mains)
* **What it shows:** The win percentage of your top 20 most-played heroes.
* **Interpretation:** A competitive race.
    * **55%+:** You are climbing MMR with this hero.
    * **<48%:** You are losing MMR with this hero.

---

## ⚔️ Gameplay & Behavior

### 4. KDA Ratio (Efficiency)
* **Formula:** `(Kills + Assists) / Deaths`
* **Interpretation:** Measures **Impact**. High KDA means you are surviving and contributing. If a hero drops rapidly here, you might be playing too aggressively/feeding.

### 5. Tower Damage (Objective Focus)
* **What it shows:** Cumulative damage dealt to buildings (in Thousands).
* **Interpretation:** Are you actually hitting buildings? Carries and Pushers (Shadow Shaman, Tiny) should dominate this list.

### 6. Total Deaths (The Feeder Board)
* **What it shows:** Cumulative number of times you have died on a hero.
* **Interpretation:** A fun "Hall of Shame." Usually dominated by squishy supports (CM, Rubick) or aggressive initiators (Axe, Huskar).

### 7. Total Damage Dealt (Millions)
* **What it shows:** Total hero damage dealt over your career.
* **Interpretation:** Shows your "Blasters." Zeus, Spectre, and Venomancer often win this race even with fewer games played.

---

## 💰 The "Grind" Stats

### 8. Total Gold Farmed (Millions)
* **What it shows:** The sheer amount of gold you have extracted from the map.
* **Fun Note:** Long-time players will see numbers in the **Billions**.

### 9. Most Purchased Items
* **What it shows:** Top 20 items you end the game with.
* **Interpretation:** Shows your "Build Habits." Watch for meta shifts (e.g., when everyone stopped buying Vladimir's Offering).

### 10. Role Evolution
* **What it shows:** A 2-bar race between **Core** and **Support**.
* **Interpretation:** The ultimate tug-of-war. Shows if you truly prefer farming (Core) or helping (Support).

### 11. Laning Preference
* **What it shows:** Which lane you started in (Safe, Mid, Off, Roam).
* **Interpretation:** Tracks your role identity shifts (e.g., "The 2016 Midlane Phase").
